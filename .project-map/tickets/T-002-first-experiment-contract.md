## Question

What must the first Codex/Herdr experiment observe and refuse when the model forgets the
collaboration procedure?

## Resolution

The first experiment targets stale or unverified results being accepted. Invalid claim,
submit, or integration state must be rejected with diagnostic evidence and a recoverable
pause; the system must not guess success. Codex is the first CLI test, with a capability
contract kept for later adapters. Herdr core changes remain evidence-driven: scale or a
reproduced public-interface gap must justify them. Evidence: user confirmations in grilling
rounds 2 and 3 and the initial research record. Closed 2026-10-05.
